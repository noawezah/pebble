# Footer moss texture

Created with the built-in imagegen tool from a description inspired by the cafe's preserved moss wall. The reference photo was not used as the background.

Assets:

- `public/images/footer-moss.webp`: 1600 × 800, WebP quality 54.
- `public/images/footer-moss-mobile.webp`: 512 × 512, WebP quality 42, center crop.

The mobile source is selected by the browser at widths up to 700 pixels. The image is lazy loaded; a dark green fallback and a subtle overlay keep the white lettering legible. Desktop uses Next.js responsive image sizing.

## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: an original landscape background texture for the footer of the PEBBLE cafe website, beneath large ivory lettering. Create a 2:1 wide, flat, front-on photographic texture of a preserved moss wall. Dense finely branched and cushion-like moss in rich dark forest green, muted fern green and deep olive, with scattered small rounded yellow-olive moss cushions similar to the natural interior moss wall in a cafe. Simple, calm overall composition, intricate realistic micro-detail and soft depth at close inspection, mostly even dark green coverage with gentle organic variation. Small olive cushions occupy only about 10 percent of the surface, understated, dispersed irregularly. Matte material, soft diffuse light, no dramatic hotspots or shadows. Edge-to-edge moss, all edges filled naturally. Keep the overall tones sufficiently dark and low contrast for white website text to be legible. This is a reusable nature texture asset, not a website screenshot. No letters, words, logos, cafe furniture, frames, panel divisions, metal bars, borders, large foliage, flowers, or watermark. Do not add a vignette; maintain a consistent material texture across the whole image.
